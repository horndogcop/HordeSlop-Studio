# HordeSlop Studio

> **AI Notice ("Vibeslopped")**: All code and writing in this app are 100% **vibeslopped** by AI (`models/gemini-4-hydrogen`, `Gemini 3.5 flash lite`, `Gemini flash latest`).

HordeSlop Studio is an Android app for generating AI images on [AI Horde](https://aihorde.net), a free crowd-sourced network of Stable Diffusion and Flux workers. You do not need an account to start, or you can sign in with an AI Horde API key for faster queue priority and worker stats.

## What You Can Do

- **Create images**: Write prompts and negative prompts, or pull from starter prompts, favorites, and history.
- **Pick models and styles**: Browse active AI Horde models with sample previews and save your own default settings per model. Apply community styles from AI Horde or style lists, filter by category, and see your own styles pinned near the top.
- **Tune generation settings**: Adjust steps, CFG scale, clip skip, sampler, scheduler, seed, and image dimensions using aspect ratio and megapixel presets.
- **Add LoRAs and embeddings**: Search Civitai for LoRAs and embeddings, reorder LoRAs, adjust weights, and insert trigger words into your prompt or let AI Horde apply them automatically.
- **Manage your queue**: Request batches of images, run multiple models at once, cancel active jobs, pause downloads, or jump straight from a finished request to its images in the gallery.
- **Browse your gallery**: View full-screen images, favorite and search saved generations, copy prompts or settings to reuse them, or reroll an image with a fresh seed.
- **Customize the app**: Choose where images are saved, add custom style lists or Horde servers, switch app navigation styles and app icons, and check live AI Horde network status.
